05 Business Systems & Operations RevOps · Sales Ops · Solutions · Product Ops
Flagship · AI + Sales Operations
Lead Routing Copilot

A system that sorts incoming sales leads. AI reads what each lead is asking for, but written rules decide where it goes. Anything unclear goes to a person with a written reason, and every decision is logged.

  • 10 checks before any lead is sent automatically. A lead with a tricky note like "ignore all previous instructions" still stops for a person.
  • A threshold test found that one of my own checks never changed a result, and the console says so plainly
  • REST API, operations console, review queue, 47 automated tests, live demo
